Our Programs

Driving Transformational Change in Cocoa

We implement evidence-based programs that create lasting impact for farmers and the environment.

Key Initiatives

Our work is focused on creating a sustainable and equitable cocoa supply chain.

Livelihoods

Prosperous Farmers

We provide farmers with the tools and knowledge to increase their productivity and income. By promoting climate-smart agriculture and financial literacy, we help families achieve resilient livelihoods.

  • Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) Training
  • Diversification for Sustainable Income
  • Access to Finance and Digital Tools
Society

Empowered Communities

Our programs address the root causes of child labor by improving access to education and strengthening community child protection systems. We empower women to take a leading role in their communities.

  • Child Labor Monitoring and Remediation (CLMRS)
  • School Construction and Quality Education
  • Women's Economic Empowerment
Environment

Healthy Planet

We are committed to halting deforestation and restoring landscapes in cocoa-growing regions. Through the Cocoa & Forests Initiative, we partner with industry and governments to protect biodiversity.

  • Forest Restoration and Reforestation
  • Support for Agroforestry Systems
  • Climate Change Mitigation and Adaptation

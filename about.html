The Organization

Who We Are

WCF is an international membership organization that promotes sustainability in the cocoa sector. WCF provides cocoa farmers with the support they need to enjoy more resilient livelihoods.

Our members include over 100 organizations representing more than 80% of the global cocoa market. Together, we work with governments, civil society, and farmers to build a multi-stakeholder movement that addresses the most critical social and environmental challenges.

Our Strategy

The WCF Strategy

We work collaboratively with our members and partners to address the most pressing environmental and social challenges in the cocoa sector.

Our focus is on three key pillars: increasing farmer income, eliminating child labor, and protecting the environment.

Our Vision

A Thriving Cocoa Sector

WCF visions a thriving cocoa sector where farmers prosper, communities are empowered, and nature is protected.

We believe that by working together, we can create a supply chain that is prosperous, inclusive, and sustainable for everyone involved.

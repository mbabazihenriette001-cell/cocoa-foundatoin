The World Cocoa Foundation (WCF) is a non-profit organization committed to improving the lives of cocoa farmers, protecting the environment, and ensuring a sustainable future for cocoa globally.

Our Mission

Empowering Communities, Protecting Nature

We work collaboratively with our members and partners to address the most pressing environmental and social challenges in the cocoa sector. Our focus is on increasing farmer income, ending child labor, and halting deforestation.

Through our global network, we implement programs that provide farmers with the tools and knowledge they need to thrive, while ensuring that the ecosystems they depend on are protected for generations to come.

Our commitment is to a cocoa supply chain that is prosperous, inclusive, and sustainable for everyone involved.

WCF works directly with farmers to improve productivity, increase income, and build resilient communities.

Increasing Productivity

Through our training programs, farmers learn modern agricultural techniques that help them produce more and better quality cocoa while using fewer resources.

  • Soil health and nutrient management
  • Integrated Pest Management (IPM)
  • Climate-smart agriculture practices

WCF is an international membership organization that promotes sustainability in the cocoa sector.
